What the games are reduced to. Each game is cut down to the money SeasonArc sees: what enters the pot before a draw, what the game tells the library to expect next, what it passes back as the last payment, and what comes back to the pot after paying. Player money is what leaves the pot: SeasonArc's size less what comes back, which is sized again later. Over a season it adds up to exactly what entered the pot. Lettery TF: $5 tickets, 20% to the treasury, 29.9% of each draw back (the 20% reseed and 30% of a 33% jackpot share, assuming the jackpot is missed, as the game itself tells the library), 40% of a missed jackpot stockpiled except every third miss, the pot and stockpile earning Aave yield weekly, and 2.5x the ticket from 69% of the season for a wallet with under 34.5% of the draws played. BullsTF: $10 tickets, 20% to the treasury, the 10% reseed back, and the same late price at 2.25x. SeedTogether: Aave yield on everything held, 10% to the treasury, income measured since the last draw and scaled to a month, planned at a cushion of 80% rising to 100%, and 20% of each sized draw held in a reserve that pays out over the remaining draws and tops up a draw that would pay less than the last.

Checked against each game's own model. BullsTF matched sim/bulls.py across flat, growing, shrinking, crashing and surging crowds, to the unit on every draw but the closing one, which differs by the cents its prize rounding leaves in the pot. Lettery TF matched sim/trace_season.py with the jackpot never won, on steady crowds of 1,000 to 50,000 (the trace holds its crowd steady), to the unit on every draw but the closing one, within $0.00003. SeedTogether, across the same five crowd shapes, matched sim/seedtogether.py to the unit on every draw but the closing one, within $0.002, and reproduces its published season ($10M from 10,000 wallets at $1,000, 4%: first draw $11,247, last $93,674).

What it leaves out. Winners, tiers and their rounding; jackpot wins; refunded draws under a minimum field. Each game's own Season Lab has its full money. The crowd is one ticket or one average deposit per player.
